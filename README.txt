JANMAT — INSIDE INDIAN DEMOCRACY
================================
A student-made, responsive, interactive civics website for an exhibition.

FILES
- index.html: page structure and content
- style.css: colours, layout and mobile responsiveness
- script.js: interactive bill tracker, House switcher, role cards, tour, quiz and glossary

RUN LOCALLY
1. Keep all three files in the same folder.
2. Double-click index.html to open it in a modern browser.
3. Internet is only needed for the optional Google Fonts import; the site otherwise runs locally.

FEATURES
- Three equally prominent organs of government
- Clickable bill journey tracker
- Lok Sabha / Rajya Sabha switcher
- Question Hour and Zero Hour explainer
- Head of State vs Head of Government interactive cards
- Judicial review and checks-and-balances examples
- Schematic clickable Parliament location map
- Three-question quiz and clickable glossary
- Responsive desktop and mobile layout

CIVICS ACCURACY NOTES
- The three organs are not three divisions inside Parliament. Parliament is part of the Union Legislature; the Executive and Judiciary have distinct constitutional roles.
- The bill tracker describes a typical path. Procedures differ by bill category; Money Bills have special rules.
- The chamber graphic and virtual tour map are schematic illustrations, not official architectural plans.
- Verify current office holders and current parliamentary details from official Parliament / government sources before presenting. This project intentionally describes offices and roles rather than relying on names that may change.

PUBLISHING
For GitHub Pages, upload index.html, style.css and script.js together to the repository's published branch/folder. Back up the old files first. Check the website on laptop and phone after publishing. This local copy does not change an already-published website automatically.

Educational simulation only; not an official Government of India website.
