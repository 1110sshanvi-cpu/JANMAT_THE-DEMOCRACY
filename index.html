ORGAN 01 / LAWMAKING

The Legislature

At the Union level, Parliament consists of the President and two Houses: Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha.

THE LOWER HOUSE

Lok Sabha

Members are directly elected by citizens. The House is central to representing the people and holding the Union government accountable.

Up to 550 elected membersConstitutional composition
Normally 5 yearsUnless dissolved earlier
SpeakerPresiding officer

The number of members actually serving can be lower than the constitutional maximum.

A bill's journey through Parliament

1IntroductionFirst Reading
2ConsiderationDebate / committee scrutiny
3VotingHouse or Houses, as required

Ordinary bills generally need agreement of both Houses. Money Bills follow special constitutional procedures.

ORGAN 02 / GOVERNING

The Executive

The Executive puts laws and public decisions into practice. In India's parliamentary system, the Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha.

01
WHO DOES WHAT?

Head of State vs Head of Government

President of India

The constitutional Head of State. Union executive actions are formally taken in the President's name, generally on the aid and advice of the Council of Ministers.

02
ACCOUNTABILITY SPOTLIGHT

Parliament can ask tough questions.

A designated period in a sitting when MPs ask ministers questions about government work, helping scrutinise administration.
A convention that allows MPs to raise matters of urgent public importance after Question Hour. Its procedures are not set out as a formal rule in the same way as Question Hour.

ORGAN 03 / JUSTICE

The Judiciary

Courts are institutionally separate from Parliament and the Executive. They interpret the Constitution and decide disputes according to law.

⚖
THE CONSTITUTIONAL CHECK

What is judicial review?

Courts can examine whether laws and government actions comply with the Constitution. A provision found unconstitutional may be struck down by a competent court.

Scenario: A law bans all peaceful public criticism.

A court could examine whether the restriction violates constitutional freedoms and whether it is legally justified. The outcome depends on the facts and constitutional tests.

SEPARATION OF POWERS

Power works best with checks.

LEGISLATURELaw-making
EXECUTIVEImplementation

Judicial independence, legislative scrutiny and constitutional limits help prevent power from becoming concentrated in one place.

Legislature scrutinises the government; the Executive is accountable to the elected House; courts can review constitutionality. These are checks, not a claim that the three organs have identical powers.
